Finite evidence, stated limits
Exact rational basis enumeration, not a scalable optimizer. Python's separate checker reconstructs all full vertices using one-, two- and three-policy simplexes and determinant minors, then checks every dual or separator inequality. Browser JSON needs that native check before use elsewhere.
Budgets are expectations under the supplied initial distribution. A rare trajectory can exceed them. One common discount is used for reward and both costs; signed costs are not automatically probabilities. The full optimal face can contain a continuum: all its vertices are listed, not all policies.
Classical occupation-measure and linear-programming theory, credited in the method and source ledger. Original implementation and teaching controls. No benchmark, safety certification or new-theory claim.
