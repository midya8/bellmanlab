01 / SPECIFY THE WHOLE MODEL

What does the budget mean?

Occupancy sums to one: x = (1 - γ) Σ γᵗ Pr(sₜ,aₜ). The budget applies to normalized expected discounted cost, not to every trajectory. Discounted totals divide by 1 - γ.

04 / A BOUND, NOT A SOFT PENALTY GUESS
Feasible plan. Matching upper bound.
Every action must satisfy v(s) ≥ r(s,a) - λ c(s,a) + γ P(s,a)·v, with λ ≥ 0. Then λ B + (1 - γ) μ·v bounds every feasible return. A zero exact gap proves optimality for this supplied model. A tied Lagrange optimum still needs a feasible primal mixture.
